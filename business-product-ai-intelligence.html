Product guide

Business Product AI Intelligence.

An introduction and reproducible walkthrough for the TAPAMS product intelligence prototype.

Business objective

Understand product age and growth signals.

The system uses invoice dates and effective dates to understand how old a product is, then highlights the areas leaders should review to improve the business.

For example, if a company has sold a mobile phone for two years, leadership can review how many customers upgraded, bought new products, or remain on the older product.

Product categories

NCNP
New Customer, New Product.
CCCP
Current Customer, Current Product.

What the intelligence means

The dashboard gives leaders signals such as Watch, which indicates that customers and current-product resilience or enhancement opportunities need review.

Steps to reproduce

Run the Intelligence

  1. First download the sample Excel File: tapams-effective-dated-sample.xlsx.
  2. Open the TAPAMS site and Sign in to TAPAMS ↗ with an email address and password.
  3. Upload the downloaded file.
  4. Review the number of customers and total value.
  5. Use the customer-category dropdown and choose a category.
  6. Review the revenue, product lifecycle, risk, and improvement signals.
  7. Upload another data file to test the intelligence with real-time sample data.

This is a test and demonstration product. Use sample or approved data only. The current prototype analyzes uploaded files locally in the browser.
